# Network Meter Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

Network Meter ("the app", Android package `com.r6tlabs.networkmeter`) is published by R6T LABS LLC (R6TLabs, "we").

## The short version

Network Meter processes network-usage information on your device. We do not receive or store your usage history on servers, and we do not sell it. The app has no internet permission and does not upload your data. You can choose to export a CSV file and share it through another app.

## What the app reads, and where it stays

- **Network traffic counters.** The app reads Android's device-wide byte counters (total and mobile) to show your current speed and record daily usage. It does not inspect the content of your traffic, the websites you visit or the networks you join.
- **Network type.** It checks whether the current connection is Wi-Fi, mobile or VPN, without reading network names or your location.
- **Per-app usage (optional).** If you grant Usage Access in Android Settings, the app reads Android's own per-app network history to show which apps use the most data. You can revoke this anytime in Android Settings.
- **App names and icons.** The app reads the names and icons of your installed apps to label the per-app chart.
- **Your settings.** Your data plan limit, reset day, theme and other preferences.

The app's recorded history and settings stay in its private storage on your device. Android's own network-usage records are read locally when you grant Usage Access. App backups are disabled.

## Exports

When you export a CSV file, the app creates it on your device and hands it to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. What happens to it after that is up to you and that app.

## Data deletion

Use Settings → Clear usage history in the app to delete its recorded daily history. This keeps your settings and the current monitoring session.

To remove all of Network Meter's local app data, including settings, clear its storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. These actions do not remove Android's system network-usage records or copies of CSV files you have already shared or saved in other apps. We do not hold a server-side copy to delete.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
